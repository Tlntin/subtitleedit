The 16 kHz inference path follows the upstream OnnxWrapper: 512 samples per
frame, 64 samples of context, float state [2, 1, 128], scalar int64 sample rate.
The model runs locally through the CPU ONNX Runtime. Audio is decoded once to
16 kHz mono PCM and streamed through VAD; each resulting speech interval is
uploaded independently and keeps its original source offset.
